2. Chunked Transfer-Encoding Translation:
Origin sends "Transfer-Encoding: chunked" without Content-Length.
Gleam caches the buffered payload and serves it on hit with computed "Content-Length: 20".

$ curl -i http://127.0.0.1:19901/chunked-no-len
HTTP/1.1 200 OK
Cache-Control: public, max-age=60
Content-Type: text/plain
Date: Sat, 03 Oct 2026 01:38:05 GMT
Server: BaseHTTP/0.6 Python/3.14.5
Transfer-Encoding: chunked

hello chunked world

$ curl -i http://127.0.0.1:19901/chunked-no-len
HTTP/1.1 200 OK
Age: 0
Cache-Control: public, max-age=60
Content-Type: text/plain
Date: Sat, 03 Oct 2026 01:38:05 GMT
Server: BaseHTTP/0.6 Python/3.14.5
Content-Length: 20

hello chunked world

3. Initial Age Header Deduction & Expiration in Redis:
Origin sends "Age: 50" with "Cache-Control: public, max-age=60".
Remaining freshness lifetime is 10 seconds (60 - 50 = 10s).

$ curl -i http://127.0.0.1:19901/initial-age-test
HTTP/1.1 200 OK
Age: 50
Cache-Control: public, max-age=60
Content-Length: 11
Content-Type: text/plain
Date: Sat, 03 Oct 2026 01:38:07 GMT
Server: BaseHTTP/0.6 Python/3.14.5

initial-age

Inspect Redis TTL immediately after caching:
$ redis-cli -p 16379 ttl "127.0.0.1:19901#/initial-age-test"
(integer) 8

Wait 10 seconds for expiration:
$ redis-cli -p 16379 ttl "127.0.0.1:19901#/initial-age-test"
(integer) -2

Subsequent request misses the cache and reaches origin:
$ curl -i http://127.0.0.1:19901/initial-age-test
HTTP/1.1 200 OK
Age: 50
Cache-Control: public, max-age=60
Content-Length: 11
Content-Type: text/plain
Date: Sat, 03 Oct 2026 01:38:22 GMT
Server: BaseHTTP/0.6 Python/3.14.5

initial-age
